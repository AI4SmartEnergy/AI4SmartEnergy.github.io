About the lab

AI4SmartGrids Lab is a research group in Griffith University’s School of Information and Communication Technology. We develop AI methods for power systems that increasingly rely on distributed solar generation and battery storage.

Our research uses data from meters, sensors and equipment records to forecast grid conditions, inform battery operation and help operators identify faults and suspicious measurements.

Research focus

  • Grid forecasting & stability
  • Battery storage & energy management
  • Solar & renewable integration
  • Fault diagnosis & grid cybersecurity

Industry collaboration

With Renox Technology, the Smart Energy Innovation Lab is developing AI for real-time grid frequency forecasting and coordinated battery response. The National Industry PhD Program also supports four doctoral places in AI-enabled power management.
