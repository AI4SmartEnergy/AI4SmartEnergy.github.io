Grid Forecasting & Stability

How early can a model anticipate a change in grid frequency, and how confidently can it forecast electricity demand?

  • Grid frequency. Study time-series models that use recent grid measurements to forecast near-term frequency changes and inform battery response.
  • Electricity demand. Investigate short-term household load forecasting under irregular consumption patterns and quantify uncertainty in the predictions.

Battery Storage & Energy Management

When should a battery charge, discharge or reserve capacity for grid support?

  • Battery dispatch. Explore how AI forecasts can inform scheduling and real-time battery decisions.
  • Operational constraints. Examine how state of charge, power limits and battery degradation affect dispatch decisions and availability for grid services.

Solar & Renewable Integration

How can local energy systems accommodate more solar generation when output varies with weather and time of day?

  • Solar forecasting. Investigate how weather forecasts and historical generation data can help predict photovoltaic output and estimate forecast uncertainty.
  • Grid and microgrid operation. Explore forecast-informed coordination of solar generation and batteries to manage local demand, peak exports and curtailment.

Fault Diagnosis & Grid Cybersecurity

When an energy system behaves unexpectedly, can AI help identify the cause and explain the evidence behind the diagnosis?

  • Fault diagnosis. Explore how telemetry, equipment records and system topology can help narrow down possible faults in solar and storage systems.
  • Secure monitoring. Study how anomaly detection and network analysis could help identify abnormal measurements and distinguish equipment faults from data or communication problems.
