16 November 2023 · National Industry PhD Program, Round 2

AI-Enhanced Resilient, Efficient, and Cyber-Secure Power Management System

The National Industry PhD Program awarded four PhD places to Griffith University and Redx Technology Australia to investigate AI methods for more efficient and secure power management. The project covers electricity demand forecasting, residential energy management, battery performance and smart grid cybersecurity.
